01 / DIVERSIFIED WEALTH CREATION

Mutual Funds & Systematic Compounding.

A professionally managed investment vehicle pooling capital from multiple investors to construct diversified portfolios of equities, debt instruments, and commodities (AMFI ARN-268488).

TWO DISTINCT APPROACHES

Mutual Funds vs Direct Equity.

APPROACH A

Mutual Funds

Capital is pooled and allocated across 40-70 securities managed by seasoned SEBI-registered fund managers with automated SIP rupee-cost averaging.

  • Instant multi-sector diversification
  • Automated monthly SIP compounding
  • Low entry threshold (from ₹500/month)
APPROACH B

Direct Equity

Direct investor ownership of specific exchange-listed corporate shares with concentrated exposure and active portfolio governance.

  • Uncapped multi-bagger upside
  • Direct corporate voting rights
  • Zero fund management expense ratio
CORE CATEGORIES

Mutual Fund Classification.

Equity Funds

Large Cap, Flexi Cap, Mid Cap & Small Cap funds for inflation-beating long-term growth.

Hybrid & Multi-Asset

Dynamic Asset Allocation and Balanced Advantage funds mitigating market drawdowns.

Debt & Liquid

Overnight, Liquid, Banking & PSU debt funds for capital safety and predictable yields.

ELSS Tax Savers

Equity Linked Savings Schemes providing Section 80C tax deductions with a 3-year lock-in.

MUTUAL FUND INVESTMENTS ARE SUBJECT TO MARKET RISKS, READ ALL SCHEME RELATED DOCUMENTS CAREFULLY.
