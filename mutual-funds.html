Mutual Funds: Systematic Compounding.

A mutual fund pools money from multiple investors and invests it in a diversified portfolio of securities according to the scheme's stated objective under full SEBI regulatory oversight.

TWO DISTINCT APPROACHES

Mutual Funds vs Direct Equity.

POOLED STRUCTURE

Mutual Funds

Capital is pooled from multiple investors and allocated across hundreds of diversified securities by a dedicated, certified Fund Manager.

STRUCTURE LIFECYCLE

Investors → Pooled Capital → Fund / Scheme → Portfolio of Securities → Investor Units

  • Professional Management: Managed according to scheme's stated mandate.
  • Instant Diversification: Spread across multiple companies, sectors, and asset classes.
  • Regulation & Transparency: Regulated by SEBI with mandatory daily NAV & monthly portfolio disclosures.
  • Automated SIP / SWP: Micro-investing from ₹500/month with zero manual order execution.

DIRECT OWNERSHIP

Direct Equity

Direct equity involves purchasing specific shares of individual listed companies on stock exchanges (NSE/BSE), holding direct corporate ownership.

STRUCTURE LIFECYCLE

Investor → Research & Analysis → Buy / Sell Shares → Ownership in Company → Long-Term Value

  • Direct Company Ownership: You own fractional equity directly in your demat account.
  • Complete Flexibility: Complete freedom to pick specific companies based on personal conviction.
  • Dividend & Growth Rights: Direct receipt of corporate dividends and voting rights.
  • Requires Ongoing Research: Demands active monitoring of earnings, quarterly concalls, and industry trends.
