Alternative Investment Funds (AIF).

Privately pooled investment vehicles governed by SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations 2012, tailored for sophisticated accredited investors seeking non-traditional alpha.

SEBI INVESTOR THRESHOLD

₹1 CRORE*

Minimum statutory investment threshold for individual investors in SEBI registered Alternative Investment Funds (₹25 Lakhs for accredited investors/employees).

CLASSIFICATION MATRIX

The Three SEBI AIF Categories.

CATEGORY I

Early-Stage & Impact

Venture Capital Funds (VCF), Angel Funds, SME Funds, and Social Venture Funds investing in startups, early-stage enterprises, and social impact models.

  • High growth potential
  • Tax pass-through status
  • 5-7 year horizon
CATEGORY II

Private Equity & Credit

Private Equity (PE) Funds, Real Estate Funds, and Structured Private Credit Funds providing growth capital and mezzanine financing to established private companies.

  • Predictable debt yields
  • Real asset collateralization
  • Tax pass-through status
CATEGORY III

Hedge & Long-Short Funds

Complex active trading strategies including Long-Short Equity, Quantitative Arbitrage, and Derivative Strategies seeking absolute alpha across market cycles.

  • Market neutral hedging
  • Leveraged trading allowed
  • Quarterly/semi-annual liquidity

AIF INVESTMENTS INVOLVE COMPLEX RISK PROFILES, LOCK-IN PERIODS, AND PRINCIPAL LOSS RISKS.
