06 / INVESTMENT SOLUTIONS

Direct Equity: Direct Enterprise Ownership.

Direct equity involves investing directly in individual listed companies, providing unbundled exposure, corporate voting rights, and uncapped capital appreciation driven by corporate earnings power.

KEY PILLARS OF DIRECT EQUITIES

Ownership & Growth Potential.

Four structural benefits of direct public equity holdings versus pooled structures.

Direct Ownership

Fractional ownership held directly in your NSDL/CDSL demat account with complete dividend credits and corporate voting rights.

Granular Customization

Freedom to choose specific companies, business models, ESG parameters, and market capitalization segments.

Compounded Growth

Direct participation in corporate earnings expansion, margin expansion, and long-term valuation rerating.

Fundamental Diligence

Demands ongoing scrutiny of quarterly financial statements, management commentary, and industry competitive dynamics.

ANALYTICAL RIGOR

How 4X Wealth Evaluates Listed Companies.

STAGE 01

Economic Moat & ROE

High Return on Equity (ROE > 18%) and Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) backed by pricing power, brand equity, or switching costs.

STAGE 02

Balance Sheet Resilience

Conservative Debt/Equity ratios, strong interest coverage, and disciplined working capital cycles across business cycles.

STAGE 03

Free Cash Flow Yield

Focus on businesses that convert accounting profits into tangible free cash flow available for reinvestment or capital return.
