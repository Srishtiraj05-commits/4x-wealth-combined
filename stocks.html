Direct Equity: Direct Ownership.

Direct equity involves investing directly in the shares of individual companies listed on stock exchanges, enabling targeted capital participation in long-term enterprise growth.

KEY FEATURES OF DIRECT EQUITIES

Ownership & Growth Potential.

Direct Ownership

You own fractional shares of companies directly in your demat account with full voting rights.

Flexibility

Freedom to choose specific companies, business models, and market capitalization segments.

Capital Growth

Direct participation in corporate earnings expansion, margin growth, and long-term valuation rerating.

Requires Research

Demands ongoing analysis, tracking quarterly filings, management changes, and industry developments.
