02 / CAPITAL PRESERVATION

Bonds & Fixed Income Securities.

Contractual debt instruments representing loans made by investors to governments or corporate borrowers, offering predictable coupon cash flows and principal preservation.

THE FIXED INCOME LIFECYCLE

How Contractual Income Works.

01. Primary Issuance

The issuer issues debt securities at face value to raise capital, defining coupon frequency, tenor, and redemption dates.

02. Regular Coupon Payments

Investors receive scheduled periodic interest payments directly into their designated bank account throughout the tenor.

03. Principal Redemption

Upon maturity, the issuer repays the original face value of the bond in full to the verified bondholder.

PRODUCT MATRIX

Fixed Income Asset Classes.

TYPE 01

Government Securities (G-Secs)

Issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Central Government. Zero sovereign default risk.

TYPE 02

State Development Loans (SDLs)

State government debt offering a 25-50 bps yield spread over Central G-Secs with RBI-managed servicing.

TYPE 03

Corporate AAA/AA Bonds

High-grade corporate debentures providing enhanced yields for institutional and private family offices.

TYPE 04

Tax-Free PSU Bonds

Public sector enterprise bonds (PFC, REC, NHAI) offering 100% tax-exempt annual coupon payments.

FIXED INCOME SECURITIES ARE SUBJECT TO INTEREST RATE RISK, CREDIT RISK, AND LIQUIDITY CONDITIONS.
