07 / INVESTMENT SOLUTIONS

Portfolio Management Services (PMS).

A SEBI-regulated, customized investment service where professional portfolio managers actively administer a client's portfolio under an agreed mandate.

SEBI INVESTOR FRAMEWORK

₹50 LAKH*

Minimum statutory aggregate investment amount applicable to new client onboarding and fresh portfolio investments under SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations.

MODEL 01

Discretionary PMS

The portfolio manager independently executes investment choices, asset allocation, and rebalancing decisions in accordance with the client's agreed investment charter without prior approval for each trade.

  • High execution agility
  • Systematic factor-based rebalancing
  • Full fiduciary mandate tracking
MODEL 02

Non-Discretionary PMS

The portfolio manager provides research, strategy, and allocation ideas, but every individual buy/sell transaction is executed strictly upon express direction and approval from the client.

  • Direct investor trade consent
  • Institutional research pipeline
  • Customized security screening
STRUCTURE SPECIFICATIONS

Key Attributes of PMS Mandates.

Structure

Managed for an individual client under an agreed mandate with direct custody in the client's demat account.

Regulation

APMI Registered (APRN00969) & SEBI PMS Regulations 2020 ensuring transparent fee disclosure and auditing.

Liquidity

Subject to the specific portfolio management agreement and secondary market equity liquidity terms.

Documentation

Portfolio management agreement, investment approach disclosure document, and audited annual performance report.

PMS INVESTMENTS INVOLVE EQUITY MARKET RISKS AND DO NOT GUARANTEE PRINCIPAL OR MINIMUM RETURNS.
