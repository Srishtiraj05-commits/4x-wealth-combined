08 / SPECIALIZED INVESTMENT FUNDS

Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).

A specialised investment structure within the mutual fund framework, introduced by SEBI to bridge the gap between Mutual Funds and PMS in portfolio flexibility.

INVESTOR FRAMEWORK

₹10 LAKH*

Minimum aggregate investment threshold across all SIF investment strategies at PAN level. The minimum investment requirement does not apply to accredited investors.
